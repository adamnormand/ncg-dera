How our team works

The process from start to finish

  1. 1

    Design

    Personalize and customize with our designers.

  2. 2

    Plan

    Plan and budget your renovation — we can help.

  3. 3

    Build

    Stay in the loop with our project managers.

1

Design

Your next amazing home renovation starts here with our experienced design team! Whether you’ve got a handful of loose ideas or a complete vision of what you want, we’ll help you get it down on paper. We’ll present you with proposals for each of your ideas, and turn the ones you like into actionable plans.

We have handled designs for so many great projects. These include kitchen renovations, bathroom renovations, basement finishing and many other jobs where homeowners needed our help to make their dreams reality. Giving you the look and feel you want for your renovation is important to us, and that’s why we work so closely with you during the design stage. We’ll introduce you to the possibilities, warn you about potential problems with your ideas and even use technology to show you what you’re getting before it’s built.

2

Plan

The planning stage follows the design stage as soon as you’re ready to hire us to build. We’ll assess your home, the designs, and the scope of the project to provide you with a free, no-obligation quote for the work. Any questions or concerns that weren’t addressed in the design stage will be covered here. We’ll take the time to establish what you want with absolute certainty.

The customized plans we create for your renovation will include the timeline, and an itemized budget. We’ll work with you directly to put the final touches on the plans—making sure all the while that the project matches the needs of your home and budget.

3

Build

In this stage, we turn all that designing and planning work into a gorgeous new look for your home. From materials to trades, we take the quality of your renovation very seriously, and we see to it that the work is protected for years to come.

Quality starts with the materials, and we are incredibly thorough when choosing the materials we source and bring to the job. All the materials we use are tested and vetted by our team. That isn’t to say that our work is only available for the highest budgets. Thanks to longstanding professional and personal relationships with our suppliers, we’re able to source quality materials at surprisingly good prices. When we save on premium materials, you save on premium renovations.

And it isn’t just our materials that are premium, either. We apply the same amount of care when choosing the team who will be handling your build. We work with experienced and certified experts, and choose only the best for each phase of the build. When the build is done, you’re protected by one of the finest warranties in the business.
